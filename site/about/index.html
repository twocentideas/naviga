About

Naviga is guided by the same principles that define good seamanship: preparation, precision, and steady navigation through complexity.

Families are difficult. Family law shouldn’t be.

After years of practicing at leading law firms in Washington, D.C. and Maine (Bernstein Shur), I counseled clients, families, and family offices as an independent, global investment management and strategic advisor at a firm with over $160B of assets under management.

I’ve built Naviga to provide direct access to experienced counsel and practical solutions, tailored to real lives and real families. It allows me to practice and work with clients on my terms, and theirs.

Naviga (the name of the sailboat I don’t own since I’m a sole practitioner law firm) reflects a deliberate departure from the traditional big-firm model—without sacrificing sophistication or rigor.

I’ve been on both sides of the table as an Attorney, but my involvement with the legal system as a Parent, Child, Mother, and Partner is what informed and built Naviga. I am a Personal Attorney.

I am licensed in the State of Maine and the District of Columbia.

How I work

Strategic, and deeply personal.

I guide individuals and families through the estate planning process with clarity, confidence, and care. My approach is strategic, but it’s also personal—protecting your assets and preparing for the future starts with understanding the people involved.

Where I focus

  • Crafting and implementing complex estate plans—revocable trusts, wills, powers of attorney, advance healthcare directives, irrevocable life insurance trusts and charitable trusts.
  • Aligning asset allocation, risk management and tax minimization with your estate planning goals.
  • Protecting vacation homes and cottages for multi-generational use.
  • Drafting and negotiating prenuptial and postnuptial agreements.
  • Establishing and managing private foundations, nonprofits and other charitable entities.
